Our Story

A Legacy Built on Stone & Trust

Since 1998, GEE. R. Granites & Marbles has been Coimbatore's trusted partner for premium natural stone. Located in the heart of Ganapathy, near Prozone Mall, we have served hundreds of homeowners, architects, and builders with an unwavering commitment to quality and craftsmanship.

Every slab we supply is handpicked for its character, durability, and beauty — because we believe the stone you choose tells the story of your space. From kitchens to commercial flooring, we deliver excellence that lasts generations.
